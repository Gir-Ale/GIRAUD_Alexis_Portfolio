Guardian

Guardian is an octopod surveillance robot built around the Theo Jansen mechanism, designed for stable locomotion over uneven terrain where wheels fall short. Powered by an ESP32 and a 2S Li-Ion battery pack, the system streams live FPV video to a custom browser-based dashboard. It features real-time pan/tilt servo control for the ESP-CAM, dynamic motor speed control, and an integrated audio speaker. The entire mechanism was CAD-modelled, iteratively refined, and 3D-printed, using an octopod configuration to ensure constant ground contact and mechanical synchronization.

ESP32-CAM C++ / Arduino WebSockets Custom PCB 3D Printing

Petal Lamp

Pétale is a modular acrylic lampshade designed to adapt to any desired ambiance (measuring 17x15x15 cm). The concept was developed in Blender with careful attention to light diffusion and petal geometry, then physically prototyped using laser cutting for precise acrylic manufacturing. The project was launched on Kickstarter (March–April 2026) and successfully raised 337% of its €176 goal, reaching €596 with 16 backers. The GIF demonstrates the lamp in use, showcasing how the blades create a warm, directional atmosphere.

Crystalboard

CrystalBoard is a hardware concept exploring the future of human-computer interaction by making online video calls more realistic and immersive. To restore lost eye contact and body language, the device uses Liquid Crystal (PDLC) films to rapidly turn the display transparent, capture a camera frame through the screen, and switch back to opaque. This creates the illusion of interacting through a clear glass window. The project encompasses extensive research into PDLC voltage driving, touch/object recognition, and a custom CAD-designed enclosure.
